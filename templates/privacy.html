Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password. When you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, such as amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to run your account, show your expense history and summaries, and keep the service working. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses third-party services to load its fonts. These services may receive your IP address when the page loads. We do not share your expense records with any other third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, contact the Spendly team at the email address listed on your account page.
